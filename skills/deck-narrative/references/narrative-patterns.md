# Narrative Arc Patterns

Choose the arc that matches what the deck is *for*, not what looks prettiest in isolation.

## 1. Executive Pyramid (`live` or `preread`)
- **Best for:** Board updates, quarterly reviews, architecture sign-offs, budget decisions.
- **Structure:**
  1. Title + one-line decision framing
  2. Executive Summary (the answer up front)
  3. Pillar 1 — Evidence / Editorial Chart
  4. Pillar 2 — Evidence / Comparison
  5. Pillar 3 — Risks & Mitigations
  6. Decision & Next Steps (owners + dates)
- **Visual bias:** Editorial D3 charts, clean stat callouts, restrained Morph zooms into key metrics.

## 2. Keynote Tension–Release (`live`)
- **Best for:** Product launches, conference talks, vision keynotes.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hero Title (calm authority, 3D or bold typographic anchor)
  2. The World Today (shared reality)
  3. The Crack / Shift (why the old way breaks)
  4. The New Mental Model (simple visual diagram)
  5. Morph Deep-Dive 1 → 2 → 3 (zooming into parts of the same mental model)
  6. Proof / Live Outcome (real metric or before/after)
  7. Closing Call to Action
- **Visual bias:** High whitespace, 3D hero anchor, strong Morph choreography across slides 4–5.

## 3. Technical Architecture Walkthrough (`live` or `preread`)
- **Best for:** Engineering reviews, developer talks, system design proposals.
- **Structure:**
  1. Title + System Scope
  2. Core Constraints & Failure Modes
  3. Full System Map (clean diagram)
  4. Morph Zoom: Component A (how it works + animated data flow)
  5. Morph Zoom: Component B (verification / reliability)
  6. Benchmarks & Trade-offs (editorial chart)
  7. Rollout Plan
- **Visual bias:** Crisp isometric/vector diagrams, Morph zooms from overview to subsystem, looping flow clips.

## 4. Startup / Product Pitch (`live`)
- **Best for:** Investors, partners, enterprise buyers.
- **Structure:**
  1. Title + Category-Defining Claim
  2. The Pain (concrete, quantified)
  3. Why Now (inflection point)
  4. The Product (hero 3D visual or live workflow)
  5. How It Works (3-step Morph progression)
  6. Traction / Proof (clean editorial chart)
  7. The Ask / Next Step
- **Visual bias:** High-craft brand identity from `DESIGN.md`, one unforgettable hero 3D visual, sharp metrics.
