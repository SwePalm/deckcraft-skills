## Overview
High-trust light-mode editorial identity inspired by financial journalism and board pre-reads.
Designed for executive clarity, calm authority, and effortless scanning.

## Colors
- **Background (`#F8FAFC`)** & **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp paper contrast with subtle slate hairlines (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Text (`#0F172A`)** & **Muted (`#475569`)**: Exceeds WCAG AA across every surface.
- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`)** & **Accent (`#059669`)**: Reserved strictly for key figures and decision callouts.

## Typography
- **Headlines (`Source Serif 4`, 36–40pt Bold)**: Editorial authority.
- **Body (`Inter`, 15–16pt Regular)**: Clean modern readability.

## Layout
- Strict `48pt` outer margin on `960×540` canvas; Pyramid structure (answer first, evidence second).

## Elevation & Depth
- Flat paper elevation using `1pt` `#E2E8F0` borders rather than heavy shadows.

## Shapes
- Subtle `10pt` rounded corners (`smoothCorners: 40`).

## Components
- **KPI Strip**: 3 clean stat cards with 48pt primary figures and muted descriptors.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** lead every slide with the executive takeaway in the headline.
- **Don't** use decorative color stripes or dark heavy shadows on light cards.
